- **Compiled:** 29 Sep 2026
- **Method note:** Every Thai instrument cited below was downloaded from gprocurement.go.th (CGD e-GP portal) or ratchakitcha.soc.go.th (`/documents/<id>.pdf` route) and read in full. Exceptions are marked. English quotes are either `[OFFICIAL-EN]`, meaning the CGD-hosted English text (the PPSA text is the Office of the Council of State translation, and the MoF Regulation and consultant MR texts are "Unofficial Translation, supported by UNDP", hosted by CGD), or `[OWN-TRANSLATION]` from the Thai Gazette text.
