# UN ESCAP RDTII 2.1 — Pillar 7: Thailand, India and Indonesia

**Research cutoff:** **31 July 2026**. Because August 2026 had not begun at the research cutoff, “in force as of August 2026” below means **in force entering 1 August 2026**; changes made later in August cannot be certified.

> **Thailand `law.go.th` re-audit:** I re-ran the Thailand search through **[www.law.go.th](https://www.law.go.th/)** / Office of the Council of State-indexed records and the stipulated Royal Gazette, PDPC, NCSA, NBTC and ETDA portals. The public `law.go.th` interface is JavaScript-driven and returned access errors to the research browser, so its indexed records were used as locators only; each reported finding still ends with a stipulated official-source URL.

### Changes produced by the Thailand re-audit

- Added the **Labour Protection Act B.E. 2541, section 115** two-year employment-record floor.
- Added the **2022 PDPC processor records-of-processing-activities notification, clause 3**.
- Added **Computer Crime Act section 19**, which court-gates the section 18(4)–(8) data-access powers.
- Refined the **2023 DPO notification** to clauses 4–6, including the 100,000-data-subject threshold and specified deemed-large-scale sectors.

## Indicative scores from the verified evidence

- **Thailand:** P7-I1 **0**; P7-I2 **0**; P7-I3 **1**; P7-I4 **0.5**; P7-I5 **1**.
- **India:** P7-I1 **0.5**; P7-I2 **0.5**; P7-I3 **1**; P7-I4 **0** as of the cutoff; P7-I5 **1**.
- **Indonesia:** P7-I1 **0**; P7-I2 **0** if binding presidential/BSSN regulations count as a dedicated horizontal framework; P7-I3 **1**; P7-I4 **0.5**; P7-I5 **1**.

India’s P7-I1 and P7-I4 results are driven by commencement: much of the DPDP Act’s substantive regime, including the Significant Data Fiduciary DPO/DPIA duties, is scheduled for **13 May 2027**, not yet operative at the cutoff. Indonesia’s general PDP implementing regulation and independent authority had still not been completed by 22 July 2026.

## Negative findings

### Thailand

- **P7-I2 — no additional controlling delta found after the `law.go.th` re-audit.** The already-held **Cybersecurity Act B.E. 2562** and the five NCSC standards notifications remain the controlling evidence of a dedicated horizontal framework and support **score 0**. The NCSA, Royal Gazette and OCS-indexed searches found no 2024–31 July 2026 instrument that displaced that result.
- **P7-I4 — no universal statutory DPIA obligation found.** I checked the PDPA, the clause 4–6 regular-monitoring/large-scale DPO notification, the state-agency DPO notification and its 2025 amendment, the security notification, breach notification and processor ROPA notification. DPO appointment remains trigger/list based; there is no general requirement for every controller to conduct a DPIA. The defensible score is **0.5**.
- **No qualifying negative result for P7-I3 or P7-I5.** The re-audit added a two-year employment-record floor and a court-gated computer-data-access provision; existing warrantless powers still drive P7-I5 to **score 1**.

### India

- **P7-I1 — no fully operative comprehensive horizontal framework at the cutoff.** The DPDP Act exists and its Board provisions were commenced, but sections 3–17 and most controller/data-principal obligations are scheduled for **13 May 2027**. The SPDI Rules 2011 consequently remained part of the operative private-sector regime because the DPDP repeal provision was also deferred. This supports **score 0.5**, not 0.
- **P7-I2 — no dedicated horizontal Cybersecurity Act found.** I checked the IT Act/CERT-In system, Telecommunications Act instruments, Telecom Cyber Security Rules, National Cyber Security Policy, RBI directions, SEBI cyber instruments and sectoral regulatory requirements. The framework is non-dedicated and/or sectoral; **score 0.5**.
- **P7-I4 — No qualifying operative provision found.** I checked DPDP Act section 10, DPDP Rules rule 13, the SPDI Rules, Telecom Cyber Security Rules, RBI IT-governance instruments, SEBI cyber instruments and insurance-sector materials. The DPDP DPO/DPIA duties do not commence until **13 May 2027**; cybersecurity officers and grievance officers are not DPOs. **Score 0 at the cutoff.**
- **No qualifying negative result for P7-I3 or P7-I5.** Multiple fixed retention floors and executive/warrantless access powers exist.

### Indonesia

- **P7-I1 — no general PDP implementing Government Regulation was adopted by the cutoff.** Komdigi was still harmonising the draft implementing regulation in 2025, and on 22 July 2026 said the separate presidential regulation establishing the independent PDP authority was still being finalised. This implementation gap does not erase the operative horizontal PDP Law, so the likely score remains **0** rather than 0.5.
- **P7-I2 — no single parliamentary Cybersecurity Act found.** Nevertheless, Presidential Regulation 47/2023 and binding BSSN Regulations 1/2024, 2/2024 and 5/2024 form a dedicated national framework for strategy, incident response, crisis management and implementation. On a legal-instrument rather than Act-only interpretation, this supports **score 0**. A methodology that insists upon a statute enacted by the legislature could instead assign **0.5**.
- **P7-I4 — no universal DPO or DPIA requirement found.** PDP Law Article 53 uses trigger conditions; Article 54 defines the officer’s DPIA role. The general implementing PP was still absent. **Score 0.5.**
- **No qualifying negative result for P7-I3 or P7-I5.** Fixed statutory retention floors and both warrantless and court-gated access mechanisms exist.
- **Former KUHAP Law No. 8 of 1981 is no longer current.** It was repealed by **Law No. 20 of 2025**, effective 2 January 2026. Searches based solely on former Articles 33–38 would therefore be outdated.
