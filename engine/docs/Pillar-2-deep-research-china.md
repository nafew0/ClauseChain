# RDTII 2.1 – Pillar 2 (Public procurement of ICT goods and digital services) – China (mainland): deep research

**Status date:** law in force as of **August 2026**. Research done **29 September 2026**; official portals were checked live that day. Later changes up to 29.09.2026 are flagged.

**Scope:** the People's Republic of China, mainland only. Hong Kong, China and Chinese Taipei are separate GPA parties and are excluded. This file records new findings beyond the five held Pillar 2 gold rows for China:
- GPL Art. 10
- GPL + Foreign Investment Law Art. 16
- MLPS Measures Art. 21 (2.1)

Held instruments appear only as (a) a different provision, (b) a subordinate or implementing instrument, or (c) a 2024–2026 currentness change. Where the brief's traps give a different indicator or score than the gold, see **Gold re-mapping notes**.

**Regime key:** GPL = Government Procurement Law regime (MOF; budget-funded entities). BL = Bidding Law regime (NDRC; works/project tendering). CIIO = critical-information-infrastructure operators. Other = outside both regimes.

## Scoring summary (held gold + new findings)

**2.1 = 1.**
- GPL Art. 10 (held) requires purchasing domestic goods and services unless they are unavailable in China on reasonable terms. Its implementing instrument, the **Imported Products Measures (财库〔2007〕119号) Arts. 4 and 7**, makes any imported purchase subject to prior finance-department approval. This is the "foreign only if domestic supply is unavailable" pattern, which scores 1.
- For ICT specifically, the **seven MOF–MIIT procurement requirement standards of 16.12.2023 (财库〔2023〕29–35号)** oblige Party and government organs from township level up to require CPUs, operating systems and databases that pass the CNITSEC **安全可靠测评** (secure-and-reliable evaluation). CNITSEC is China's state IT-security test centre.
  - The CNITSEC Guideline V4.0 (30.06.2026) admits only **"entities registered in China"** whose design, development and production are done in China.
  - All nine result lists (Dec 2023 to Sep 2026) contain only Chinese products.
  - This is a category exclusion that only domestic products can meet: 1 [ALSO-10.1].
- Two separate 0.5-tier named-firm exclusions add to this:
  - **财库〔2026〕10号 (22.06.2026)**, which bars government purchase of products of **46 named US firms**;
  - the **CAC Micron decision (21.05.2023)** [CIIO-SCOPE].

**2.2 = 1 (Medium-High confidence).**
- The **Operating System Procurement Requirement Standard (财库〔2023〕34号)** makes "*代码无风险" a starred (mandatory) indicator that every GPL purchaser must write into its tender. Its server-OS wording reads "操作系统厂商提供源代码，源代码可供第三方机构审查" (the OS vendor provides source code for third-party review).
- Separately, for Party and government buyers, passing the CNITSEC evaluation is a condition of winning. That evaluation requires the applicant to hold and submit "代码数据" (code data) and "研发环境" (R&D environment) and to undergo "代码审查" (code review) at a state test centre.
- **Fallback: 0.5.** The same standards mandate SM2/SM3/SM4 national cryptography (GM/T 0002/0003/0004) and certified crypto modules for PCs, servers, operating systems and databases. That alone scores 0.5.
- The gold's 2.2 = 0 rested on MLPS/GB/T 22239 and said "no requirements for source code … or specific encryption … to win a tender". That is no longer correct.

**2.3 = 1.**
- The **State Council General Office notice 国办发〔2025〕34号 (in force 01.01.2026)** gives **本国产品 (domestic products) a 20% price deduction** in evaluation against non-domestic products.
  - The test is origin: products must be produced in China, i.e. substantially transformed within the PRC customs territory.
  - It discriminates by origin even though foreign-invested enterprises (FIEs) that manufacture in China qualify. Under trap step 2 it scores 1.
- The SME price deduction (10–20%) and reserved shares, the prison-enterprise preference and the disabled-welfare-unit preference are each 0.5-tier status preferences. Together they also reach 1.
- Institutional transparency is present, so the transparency limb does not add to the score. See the transparency row for the caveat about unpublished xinchuang directives.

**2.4 = 1.** On wto.org, China is a GPA **observer since 21 February 2002**, marked "Pending GPA accession negotiations". It is not a party.

**Weighted Pillar 2 result (40/32/20/8): 1.00.** If 2.2 falls back to 0.5, the result is **0.84**.

## Gold re-mapping notes

1. **GPL Art. 10 (gold: 2.1 = 1).** Agreed. Art. 10 is mandatory ("应当采购本国货物、工程和服务", "shall procure domestic goods, works and services"), not merely enabling. Add as its implementing pinpoint the Imported Products Measures 财库〔2007〕119号 Arts. 4 and 7 (row 1).
   - The domestic-product definition that Art. 10 left to the State Council now exists in 国办发〔2025〕34号. That notice works as a **price preference (2.3)**, not an exclusion.
   - The 2.1 exclusion stays with the Art. 10 + 119号 approval route and with the ICT xinchuang standards.
2. **MLPS Measures Art. 21 (gold: 2.1 = 1).**
   - Correct the title to 《信息安全等级**保护**管理办法》 (公通字〔2007〕43号).
   - The rule binds every Level-3+ system operator, public or private, and covers information-security products only. It is not a procurement-law measure, so it fits better as [ALSO-10.1] / context.
   - China's ICT 2.1 = 1 should rest on the 2023 MOF–MIIT standards plus the CNITSEC guideline (rows 2–4), which are GPL measures. Keep the MLPS row as supporting evidence.
3. **GB/T 22239-2019 cl. 8.1.9.3 (gold: 2.2 = 0).**
   - The 0 score for that clause stands. The gold's comment "there are no requirements for source code disclosure or for using specific encryption in order to win a tender" is **wrong from 16.12.2023**.
   - Under 财库〔2023〕34号 the OS vendor must provide source code for third-party review (a starred, substantive tender requirement).
   - Under 财库〔2023〕29–35号, SM-algorithm and certified-crypto items are starred as well.
   - The CNITSEC evaluation, mandatory for Party and government buyers, includes code submission and code review. **Re-score 2.2 to 1** (fallback 0.5).
4. **SME Measures Art. 3 (gold: 2.3 = 1).**
   - Under the brief, an SME preference open to any SME registered in China, including FIEs, scores **0.5**, not 1. Art. 2's test is registration in China, not ownership.
   - China's 2.3 = 1 should rest on the **origin-based 20% domestic-product deduction** (国办发〔2025〕34号 Sec. 三). Independently, it also rests on ≥2 × 0.5: the SME, prison-enterprise, disabled-unit and energy-saving preferences.
5. **GPL + FIL Art. 16 (gold: 2.1 = 0).** Agreed as context. Its protection extends only to FIEs' products **made in China**, which is why the origin-based measures still score. Add FIL Implementing Regulations Arts. 15–17 and 财库〔2021〕35号 as supporting context (rows 24–25).

- **No published instrument makes the Unreliable Entity List or AFSL countermeasures reach government procurement** apart from 财库〔2026〕10号. Reference: GPL Art. 10.

- **No ownership or nationality test in the SME definition**: FIEs registered in China qualify (财库〔2020〕46号 Art. 2). Reference: GPL Art. 9.
- **No foreign-controlled cloud on the CAC cloud-assessment pass list** (11.09.2026), and **no foreign CPU, OS or database on any CNITSEC result list**. Both are de facto effects with no written bar. Reference: GPL Art. 22.
