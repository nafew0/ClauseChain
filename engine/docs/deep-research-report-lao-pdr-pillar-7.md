**Negative findings:** 

- **P7-I4 (DPO/DPIA):** Laos’s EDP law (2017) does not impose a general mandatory DPO or DPIA duty on all organisations. No universal DPO/DPIA requirement was found beyond sectoral or later administrative provisions. The closest measures are specialized industry guidance (e.g. proposed rules for telecom or finance), but no broad statutory obligation. Thus a general DPO/DPIA requirement is absent (score 0.5).  

- **P7-I3 (retention, beyond AML):** Apart from AML five-year rules, no other horizontal retention floor was identified. We found no explicit fixed retention periods in Laos’s tax, company, health, or labor laws that directly match the Pillar 7 criteria.  

- **P7-I2 (cybersecurity framework):** In addition to the new Cybersecurity Law (2026), we found no other horizontal cybersecurity statutes prior to that. Sectoral cyber regulations (e.g. related to IT security) exist but no earlier comprehensive law. The new law in 2026 establishes the dedicated framework.  

**Could not verify on official portal:** 

- The text of Laos’s 2026 Cybersecurity Law is reported in secondary sources but we could not locate the official gazette PDF in time. We relied on the Gazette announcement and media reports.  

- The AML Law provisions were confirmed via the AML Intelligence Office site. Laos Official Gazette confirmation of these specific articles was not directly retrievable via the prescribed portals.  

- Official Lao sources (gazette or NA) for some sectoral statutes (e.g. companies act, tax code retention, labor code retention) were not directly accessible; we focused on AML and criminal-procedure sources which were confirmed.
