# RDTII 2.1 Pillar 2 (Public procurement of ICT goods and digital services): deep research on Timor-Leste

**Law as in force in August 2026. Research done 29 September 2026.** Every Jornal da República PDF, WTO page and government page cited below was fetched and checked with `curl -I` (HTTP 200) on 29 Sep 2026. All quotes from Portuguese are my own translations and are marked [OWN-TRANSLATION]. Quotes from WTO documents are the official English text and are marked [OFFICIAL-EN]. "p." means the page number printed in the Jornal issue. ESCAP holds no gold data for Timor-Leste, so this is a full sweep of all four indicators.

**Key correction to the brief.** The regime that DL 1/2025 replaced is **not** DL 10/2005 and DL 11/2005. It is **Decreto-Lei n.º 22/2022**, de 11 de maio (Regime Jurídico do Aprovisionamento, dos Contratos Públicos e das Respetivas Infrações), which applied from 1 Jan 2023. DL 22/2022 Art. 193.º had already revoked DL 10/2005, DL 11/2005 and DL 12/2005. DL 1/2025 Art. 4.º then revoked DL 22/2022, with effect from 1 Jan 2026. The currentness rows below reflect this.

**Verified dates.**
- **DL 1/2025:** originally due to enter into force on 1 Jun 2025 (Art. 5.º). **DL 25/2025**, de 13 de agosto, moved this to **1 Jan 2026**, with effect from 1 Jun 2025 (Série I N.º 33, pp. 840–841).
- **Implementing regulation:** the Council of Ministers approved it on 22 Dec 2025. It was published as **Decreto do Governo n.º 1/2026**, de 23 de janeiro (Série I N.º 3 B, Número Extraordinário, pp. 1–90), in force 1 Jan 2026.
- **Second decree (Documentos Padrão):** the government press release says a second decree approving standard documents was adopted on the same day. It was **not found** in the Jornal index or on the Ministry of Finance (MoF) site (see "Could not verify on official portal").

## Scoring summary (proposed)

| Indicator | Proposed score | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|
| **2.1** Foreign exclusions | **0** | DL 1/2025 Art. 28.º n.º 1 lets any natural or legal person, or consortium, bid in every procedure. There is no national/international tender split and no national-only tier below any threshold. The only exclusion found, the DL 22/2022 Art. 49.º reservation to nationals below US$100,000, was revoked for procedures started from 1 Jan 2026. It survives only for procedures started earlier (see caveat). |
| **2.2** Source code / encryption / trade secrets | **0** | No surrender of source code, escrow, patents or trade secrets, and no mandated encryption, is a tender condition. IP transfer is only a *negotiable* item (Art. 77.º n.º 5 h)). The rules protect bidders' IP and trade secrets. The qualified e-signature needed for submissions is authentication, and DL 12/2024 recognises foreign certificates of equivalent reliability. The standard contracts could not be checked for commissioned-software IP clauses. |
| **2.3** Limitations | **1** | DL 1/2025 Arts. 86.º–87.º impose a **mandatory** price/score uplift of up to **10%** for "fornecedores nacionais". That status is defined by nationality or by >50% national ownership (Art. 4.º q)). Non-nationals meeting the same local-content tests get at most **5%**. This is a limitation that discriminates by nationality, which scores 1 under trap step 2. It is backed by the regulation's worked examples and reinforced by local-content conditions (Art. 88.º; local-content plan required at ≥US$500,000) and by MSME cost advantages. The transparency limb is not triggered: the laws and regulation are publicly available, although the standard bidding documents are not verifiably published. |
| **2.4** Not in GPA | **1** | Timor-Leste is a GPA **observer** since 27 Sep 2024, not a party. In its WTO Working Party report (WT/ACC/TLS/34 para. 255) it committed to apply for GPA membership within one year of accession. Negotiations were launched in 2025, but WTO reported no initial market-access offer as of 24 Jun 2026. CPC 752/754/84 coverage: none. |

Main caveats:
1. **DL 22/2022 Art. 49.º still applies** to procedures started before 1 Jan 2026. An evaluator who counts pending procedures could score 2.1 = 1.
2. **Supplier Register rule is ambiguous.** Regulation Anexo I Art. 5.º n.º 3 a) could be read as requiring Timorese company registration to join the Register. Under that reading, restricted tenders (including custom software, AI and data platforms) and requests for quotations would be closed to unregistered foreign firms, which is a possible 2.1 exclusion. Anexo 4 of the same decree accepts a home-country licence and tax number, so I read it as open.
3. **Standard contracts (Documentos Padrão) are not published** on any official portal I could reach, so the [2.2-COMMISSIONED-IP] check is incomplete.

## Negative findings

Each item cites the article of the general procurement law used as the reference, under ESCAP's "lack of measures" rule.

1. **2.1: no outright or category exclusion of foreign firms under the Code.** Any natural or legal person, or consortium, may bid (DL 1/2025, Code Art. 28.º n.º 1). Equal access is guaranteed (Art. 6.º).
2. **2.1: no national-only procedure below any threshold.** The Code has no "concurso internacional" versus national-tender distinction, and none of the procedures (public tender, restricted tender, request for quotations, direct award) is limited to nationals (Code Arts. 35.º–38.º, 41.º). The old national-tender reservation (DL 10/2005 regime) and the discretionary DL 22/2022 Art. 49.º reservation are repealed for new procedures.
3. **2.1: no "foreigners only if no national supplier is available" rule** anywhere in the Code or regulation (Code Arts. 28.º, 41.º–42.º).
4. **2.1 [ALSO-10.1]: no government-only ban on a foreign vendor, app or cloud** was found in the Code, the regulation, the TIC TIMOR diploma or the Jornal indices (reference: Code Art. 28.º n.º 1). WT/ACC/TLS/34 para. 244 notes that an old brand-preference circular for Mitsubishi/Toyota vehicles (Circular Note 003/GPM/III/2009) was revoked.
5. **2.1/2.3: no mandatory consortium or partnering with nationals.** Consortia are optional (Code Art. 28.º n.os 1–5). Consortia count toward the preference only if made up entirely of national suppliers (Art. 4.º q) iv).
6. **2.2: no source-code, escrow, patent or trade-secret surrender condition.** Searches of the Code and regulation for «código-fonte», «código fonte», «fonte» and «depósito» (escrow) found nothing relevant. IP transfer is negotiable only (Code Art. 77.º n.º 5 h)), and bidders' IP and trade secrets are protected (Regulation Anexo I Arts. 11.º n.º 5 and 21.º n.º 22).
7. **2.2: no mandated national or government-certified encryption for bidders' products.** Reference: Code Art. 23.º (agency systems only). DL 12/2024 Arts. 6.º and 18.º (free choice of certifier; foreign certificates recognised).
8. **2.3 / P6: no data-residency or national-data-centre condition as a bid criterion.** Reference: Code Arts. 86.º–88.º (the only listed conditions are local content, employment, technology transfer and local sourcing). DL 29/2017 is government-only.
9. **2.3: no SME set-aside or reservation.** Reference: Code Arts. 104.º and 112.º (guarantee and payment-term advantages only). DL 30/2023 has no procurement provision.
10. **2.3: no quota on foreign goods or suppliers** (Code Arts. 86.º–88.º).
11. **Budget execution 2026:** «Decreto-Lei n.º 41/2025, de 15 de dezembro — Execução do Orçamento Geral do Estado para 2026» (Série I N.º 50 D, p. 1ff), Art. 5.º, only asks entities to start procurement early and requires a National Development Agency (ADN) opinion for works above US$200,000. There is no national preference (reference: Code Art. 86.º).
12. **Cybercrime law:** no cybercrime or cybersecurity law appears in the Jornal's Leis do Parlamento index for 2022–2026 or its Decretos-Leis index (checked 29 Sep 2026). Resolução do Governo n.º 39/2026 (Série I N.º 32 C) is a national action plan against online scam and cyber-fraud networks: policy, with no procurement condition. Reference: Code Art. 28.º n.º 1.
13. **Post-cut-off note:** «Decreto-Lei n.º 27/2026, de 4 de setembro — Aprova o Regime Excecional de Contratação Pública aplicável ao projeto do Centro Internacional de Convenções» (exceptional procurement regime for the International Convention Centre; Série I N.º 35 C, pp. 1–3) is after the August 2026 as-of date. It is construction-led [NON-ICT-SECTOR] and allows direct award. No nationality clause was seen. Not scored.
