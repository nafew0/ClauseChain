## Negative findings  
- **Mongolia, P7-I4:** No economy-wide **unconditional** DPO or DPIA requirement was found. The 2021 data-protection law and its regulations do not impose a general mandatory DPO or DPIA duty for all controllers; only limited record-keeping and security measures are prescribed.  
- **Mongolia, P7-I5:** Could not identify specific warrantless vs. court-ordered data-access powers from available official texts. (We searched relevant criminal, procedural and sector laws on the official portals without finding explicit provisions. Known access powers appear embedded in codes like the *Criminal Procedure Law* and *Law Enforcement Acts*, but exact clauses were not retrieved for confirmation.)
