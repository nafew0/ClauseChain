**Negative findings:** No general personal data protection law or comprehensive cybersecurity law was found in Timor-Leste’s legislation. No horizontal statute mandates data-security obligations or “keep no longer than necessary.” No sector-specific telecom/ISP retention laws were identified beyond what is cited above. 

**Could not verify on official portal:** None (all cited instruments are available on official government portals).

Could not verify on the stipulated official portal
Código de Processo Penal (Criminal Procedure Code): While secondary sources indicate that the CPP contains provisions on the interception of computer data and search/seizure requiring judicial warrants, the current consolidated official PDF on the Ministry of Justice portal could not be text-searched reliably for the exact article numbers governing digital evidence access. Therefore, only the explicit AML Law provision (Art. 33) was cited for P7-I5.
Draft Cybersecurity/Cybercrime Laws: Mentions of a "Lei de Cibersegurança" or "Lei do Cibercrime" appear in news outlets and government press releases (e.g., mid-2024 to 2026), but no official bill text or gazette notice was found on mj.gov.tl or parlamento.tl.
