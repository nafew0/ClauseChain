# RDTII 2.1: Pillar 6 (Cross-border Data Policies), Democratic Republic of Timor-Leste

**Status date:** August 2026. Research was completed on 28 September 2026.
**Scope:** full sweep. The Round 2 database has no Timor-Leste sheet, so nothing was skipped.
**Method:**
- Legal texts were read from the *Jornal da República* PDFs hosted on mj.gov.tl, the official gazette archive of the Ministry of Justice. Full-text keyword scans were run on those PDFs, and each hit was then read in context.
- Treaty texts come from the authentic English versions annexed to the Parliament resolutions published in the *Jornal da República*, and from asean.org and wto.org.
- Secondary sources were used only as leads. Where they are cited, this is flagged.

**Quote labels:**
- [OFFICIAL-EN]: authentic or official English text (UNTAET regulations; treaty texts published in the JR).
- [GOV-UNOFFICIAL-EN]: English published on a government portal.
- [OWN-TRANSLATION]: my translation, with the Portuguese original given alongside.

**Other flags:**
- [2024–26 CHANGE]: a new or changed instrument from 2024–2026.
- [GOV-ONLY], [DRAFT – NOT SCORED], [OUT-OF-SCOPE – network], [NOT YET IN FORCE]: as defined in the brief.

## 2. Negative findings (instrument, articles checked, result)

**Horizontal and privacy**
- **Constitution (2002):** arts. 36, 37 and 38 checked. No transfer or location clause, and art. 38(2) has never been implemented.
- **Civil Code (*Lei n.º 10/2011*; JR Série I, N.º 34, 14 Sep 2011):** arts. 67–78 (personality rights; art. 77 on privacy) checked. No transfer clause.
- **Penal Code (*Decreto-Lei n.º 19/2009*):** arts. 183–187 (crimes against private life) and 268–269 (computer fraud) checked, headings verified. No transfer clause. The article bodies were only partly read.
- **Consumer Protection Law (*Lei n.º 8/2016*; JR Série I, N.º 26 A, 8 Jul 2016):** arts. 1–33 checked. No data clause.
- **Personal data protection law:** none in force. The Leis list (mj.gov.tl, to Lei 10/2026) and the Decretos-Leis list (to DL 26/2026) were checked.
- **Cybercrime or cybersecurity law:** none in force, on the same lists. The "Lei n.º 5/2024" lead is wrong: that law concerns the mandates of officials designated by Parliament. Lei n.º 15/2011 (international judicial cooperation in criminal matters) was only partly read: law-enforcement cooperation, with no data-location clause.

**Digital and e-commerce**
- **DL n.º 12/2024:** arts. 1–82 keyword-scanned; arts. 5, 6, 8, 12, 30, 35, 39, 46 and 52 read. No storage-location, local-server or transfer clause (row 10).

**Telecom**
- **DL n.º 15/2012:** full text scanned (arts. 1–88; art. 49 read). No location clause (row 13).
- **DL n.º 31/2024:** amended arts. 13, 30 and 61 read. Negative.
- **Decreto do Governo n.º 9/2008:** arts. 6–9 read. Retention periods only.
- **ANC SIM-registration guidelines (§5) and ANC registration guidelines (§§4, 6, 8, 20.4, 37):** no location clause.

**Finance**
- **UNTAET Reg. 2000/8:** full text read. The only location rule is s. 27.2 (row 1). s. 20 (secrecy) and s. 35 (inspection; foreign supervisors' on-site inspections) are not 6.4.
- **Lei n.º 10/2026:** full 114 pp. scanned; arts. 51, 65–68, 78 and 104 read (rows 6–8).
- **BCTL Organic Law (*Lei n.º 5/2011*):** arts. 29, 31, 35 and 74 checked. No location or transfer clause; art. 74(2)(d) covers supervisory cooperation only.
- **AML/CFT law (*Lei n.º 17/2011*, JR Série I, N.º 46, 28 Dec 2011, as amended by Lei n.º 5/2013):** art. 15 (record keeping, 5 years, "available for consultation by the UIF"): **no location specified**. Arts. 27 and 46 cover cooperation between authorities.
- **DL n.º 16/2014 (FIU):** arts. 11–12 checked. [GOV-ONLY] (row 8).
- **UNTAET Reg. 2000/5 (currency exchange bureaux):** s. 6 and s. 9 checked. Registers required, with no location.
- **Lei n.º 6/2005 (insurance):** arts. 8, 26, 32, 34 and 52 checked (row 4).

**Corporate and tax**
- **Lei n.º 10/2017:** arts. 2, 3, 9, 87 and 93 checked (row 5).
- **Commercial Registry (*DL n.º 16/2017*, JR Série I, N.º 19):** arts. 3, 5, 14–15, 20 and 22 checked. Filing with SERVE (the business registration service) is disclosure to a domestic authority; no location or transfer clause. It was amended by DL n.º 37/2023, which I did not check.
- **Taxes and Duties Act (*Lei n.º 8/2008 – Lei Tributária*; JR Série I, N.º 26, 30 Jun 2008; amended by Lei n.º 5/2019):** records must be kept in an official or working language (Tetum, Portuguese, English or Indonesian) for at least 5 years. **There is no rule on where they are kept**, so this is a language and retention rule only.
- **UNTAET Reg. 2000/18:** s. 62.2 (official language, 5 years; no location).
- **VAT:** no VAT law yet. It is still a draft aimed at 2027.
- **Private Investment Law (*Lei n.º 15/2017*; JR Série I, N.º 33):** arts. 18, 23 and 45 checked. Negative; art. 18 guarantees free transfer of funds, not data.

**Statistics, health and identity**
- **DL n.º 17/2003:** arts. 1–10 checked.
- **DL n.º 4/2023:** arts. 4, 22 and 25 checked.
- **Health System Law (*Lei n.º 10/2004*, as amended by Lei 24/2021):** arts. 7(1)(d) and 22 checked. Confidentiality only.
- **DL n.º 2/2004:** arts. 23–36 checked. All [GOV-ONLY] or negative.
- **Unique ID (SIDU, the single digital identity system):** policy stage only (Council of Ministers, 27 Aug 2025). No legal instrument yet.

**Payments**
- ***DL n.º 17/2015 – Sistema Nacional de Pagamentos* (National Payment System; JR Série I, N.º 23, 24 Jun 2015):** arts. 1–6 read. No location clause **in the part read**. Arts. 7–21 could not be read (see §3).

## 5. Provisional score sheet

| Indicator | Provisional score | Decisive row / negative finding |
|---|---|---|
| **P6-I1** Ban / local processing | **0** | Negative. There is no outright ban or mandatory domestic processing anywhere in force. The strongest data rule, Lei 13/2005 art. 25(2), *expressly* allows petroleum data to be "livremente exportados" (freely exported). There is no personal data protection law (row 11 is a draft). |
| **P6-I2** Local storage (copy) | **1** | **At least two sectoral categories.** Row 1: UNTAET 2000/8 s. 27.2 (bank customer and credit records at the head office in Timor-Leste). Row 3: Lei 12/2021 art. 55(1)(c) (mining records, originals in Timor-Leste). Both are mandatory. Supported by row 2 (petroleum, discretionary) and row 4 (insurance). This stays 1 after 12 Aug 2027 because rows 2–4 and row 6 remain. |
| **P6-I3** Infrastructure precondition | **0** | Negative. No local-server or data-centre precondition in telecom (DL 15/2012), e-commerce (DL 12/2024, where art. 8(2)(b) makes server location irrelevant) or banking (Lei 10/2026 art. 104). BCTL Instruções 29/2025 and 30/2025 are unverified (§3). |
| **P6-I4** Conditional flow regime | **0** | Negative. No personal-data or sectoral transfer-abroad regime is in force. Bank and telecom secrecy and consent rules are not specific to transfers (rows 7 and 13). The only cross-border conditions are authority-to-authority [GOV-ONLY] (row 8). **Watch:** the personal data protection draft, which could move this to 1. |
| **P6-I5** No binding data-transfer agreement | **0 (panel decision needed; alternative 1)** | Row 18: ATISA FS Annex Art. 9(1)(a) is a "shall not prevent transfers of information" rule, approved by Res. PN 45/2025 and in transition for Timor-Leste from 1 Jan 2026. On the brief's own test, that makes it at least one binding agreement in force. **Alternative 1:** if the panel counts only horizontal or e-commerce data-flow commitments (AAEC, RCEP, DEPA-type), Timor-Leste has none: AAEC not acceded and soft, DEFA unsigned, RCEP not joined. |

**Main open risks:**
1. BCTL Instruções 29/2025 and 30/2025 (possible 6.3 or 6.4 finding).
2. Confirmation that ATISA was in the 24 Nov 2025 deposit.
3. Whether the personal data protection law is adopted in late 2026, which would change the Round 3 picture.
