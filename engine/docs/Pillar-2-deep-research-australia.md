## 3. Negative findings

Unless stated, each item cites the general procurement law used as the reference row: **Commonwealth Procurement Rules 17 November 2025 (F2025L01263)**, made under PGPA Act s 105B(1).

1. **Above the threshold (from $125k, or $400k for prescribed corporate entities, or $7.5m for construction) there is no outright bar on foreign firms or foreign goods.** Reference: CPR para 10.2 (no discrimination by "degree of foreign affiliation or ownership, location, or the origin of their goods and services"). Finance's FAQ confirms that the Australian-business definition "cannot" be used above the thresholds.
2. **No local-presence, local-registration, local-agent or joint-venture requirement in the Commonwealth rules.** Reference: CPR 10.14 (conditions for participation limited to legal, commercial, technical and financial abilities), 10.15 (no requirement of prior experience "in a particular location") and 10.16(a) (abilities assessed "wherever they have occurred"). AusTender registration makes ABN optional (R40). The DMP2 Panel Agreement Part A (March 2024) has no Australian-incorporation eligibility clause. Its cl. 26.2 security-clearance duty is a personnel rule.
3. **No domestic price-preference margin in Commonwealth procurement.** Reference: CPR 10.2 and 4.4–4.6 (value for money). Para 4.7 is an evaluation consideration, not a margin (R22).
4. **No mandatory local-content percentage at the Commonwealth level.** Reference: CPR 4.7–4.8. The CAIP policy states it "does not mandate" use of Australian industry (R23). Local-content floors exist only at state level (Victoria, R33).
5. **No offsets or industrial-collaboration obligation on foreign suppliers in civil Commonwealth procurement.** Reference: CPR 10.2. The Defence AIC program is reserved under the GPA but was not verified on a listed portal (R32, §4).
6. **No surrender of the supplier's pre-existing source code, patents or trade secrets as a tender condition.** Reference: CPR 10.6 (request documentation must set out any conditions for participation) and 10.14. The CCS default IP clause favours the supplier (R16), the IP Principles default to supplier ownership of software (R17), and escrow in DTA templates and DMP2 is optional (R14, R15).
7. **No national (non-international) encryption algorithm is mandated for bidders.** Reference: CPR 10.10(b) (specifications based on international standards). The ASD-approved algorithms are NIST/FIPS standards. The only government-certified cryptography requirement is HACE for SECRET/TOP SECRET (R12).
8. **The Future Made in Australia Act 2024 (C2024A00119) does not reach procurement.** Reference: CPR 2.9(a), under which grants are not procurement. The Act s 10(2) defines "Future Made in Australia support" as "a grant, loan, indemnity, guarantee, warranty, investment of money or equity investment", and the word "procure" does not appear in the Act.
9. **The Australian Jobs Act 2013 has not been amended since the October 2024 version the gold holds.** Its latest compilation is C2024C00577 (14 Oct 2024), with no unincorporated amendments at 29 Sep 2026. The only change is the AIP Rules 2026 remake (R24). Reference: CPR 4.9 (procurement-connected policies).
10. **No Commonwealth CPR edition later than 17 Nov 2025.** The register lists F2025L01263 as the only in-force CPR instrument. F2024L00627 is "Repealed", and F2025L01263 has compilation 0 with no amendments. Reference: CPR para 1 (foreword, repeal clause).
11. **No government-only ban on foreign cloud or hosting providers as such.** The HCF is a certification with ownership-stability conditions, not a nationality bar (R26). Reference: CPR 8.3 (procurement security risk managed under the PSPF).
12. **The DTA Contract Limits and Reviews Policy (1 Feb 2020) and Fair Criteria Policy (1 Jul 2019) are nationality-neutral.** The first sets a $100m cap and a three-year initial term. The second is a pro-competition checklist. Reference: CPR 5.1–5.2 (encouraging competition).
13. **The Secure Australian Jobs Code and Framework are not in force.** They are a draft, with consultation closing 21 Oct 2026, so they are excluded. Only the enabling Fair Work Act amendment is in force (R31). Reference: CPR 6.13 (Supplier Code of Conduct, the current instrument).
14. **The Workplace Gender Equality compliance letter and the Payment Times procurement-connected policy are neutral compliance conditions.** The WGEA letter applies to employers with 100 or more employees in Australia; Payment Times applies to contracts above $4m. Neither limits foreign bidders. Reference: CPR 4.9.
